# Toxin analyses performed by Greg Boyer's lab 
In October 2024 we submitted our GFF and PC filters from our two Lake Erie CSMI cruises from August and September. We submitted the volumes filtered as well as the filters to be analyzed by Greg Boyer's lab at SUNY ESF.

In Februrary 2025 we received the results from these analyses and they are in both an .xlsx and .pdf file. While we did not have any cylindrospermopsin (typcial for Great Lakes) we do have microcystin and anatoxins present although they are lowly abundant. We find that the majority of toxins were present in the West Basin of Lake Erie and anatoxins were present in West Basin but nearshore around Point Pelee and Sandusky Bay (I have to double check the Sandusky Bay part though because its that station area across from Point Pellee)
